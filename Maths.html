Maths - Class 10

1. Real Numbers

Real numbers include rational and irrational numbers. They can be represented on a number line.

Rational Numbers

Numbers that can be written in the form p/q are called rational numbers, where q is not zero.

Examples: 1/2, 3/4, 5, -2

Irrational Numbers

Numbers that cannot be written in the form p/q are called irrational numbers.

Examples: √2, √3, π

2. Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics in which letters and symbols are used to represent numbers.

Important Terms

  • Variable - A letter that represents a number.
  • Constant - A fixed number.
  • Coefficient - A number multiplied by a variable.
  • Expression - A combination of numbers and variables.
Example: 3x + 5

Algebraic Identity

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

3. Quadratic Equations

An equation of the form ax² + bx + c = 0 is called a quadratic equation, where a is not zero.

ax² + bx + c = 0

Quadratic Formula

x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a
Example: x² - 5x + 6 = 0

4. Matrices and Determinants

A matrix is a rectangular arrangement of numbers in rows and columns.

Example of Matrix

[ 1   2 ]
[ 3   4 ]

The matrix above has 2 rows and 2 columns.

Order of Matrix

Order = Number of Rows × Number of Columns

5. Sequences and Series

A sequence is an ordered list of numbers following a particular pattern.

Arithmetic Sequence

In an arithmetic sequence, the difference between consecutive terms is constant.

2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ...

Common Difference

d = Second Term - First Term

6. Geometry

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with shapes, sizes, angles and properties of figures.

Triangle

A triangle has three sides and three angles.

Sum of angles of a triangle = 180°

Important Topics

  • Triangles
  • Congruent triangles
  • Similar triangles
  • Angles
  • Geometrical theorems

7. Coordinate Geometry

Coordinate geometry uses numbers to locate points on a plane.

Coordinates

A point is written as (x, y).

Example: (3, 4)

Distance Formula

d = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]

8. Trigonometry

Trigonometry is the study of relationships between angles and sides of triangles.

Basic Ratios

sin θ = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Perpendicular / Base

Example

For a right-angled triangle, trigonometric ratios help us find unknown sides or angles.

9. Statistics

Statistics is the study of collecting, organizing and analyzing data.

Mean

Mean = Sum of all values / Number of values

Median

Median is the middle value of an ordered data set.

Mode

Mode is the value that occurs most frequently.

Example: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5
Mode = 3

10. Probability

Probability tells us how likely an event is to happen.

Formula

Probability = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

Example

A coin has two possible outcomes: Head and Tail.

Probability of getting a Head = 1/2
