Grammar

Parts of Speech

  • Noun - Name of a person, place, animal or thing
  • Pronoun - Used instead of a noun
  • Verb - Shows an action or state
  • Adjective - Describes a noun
  • Adverb - Describes a verb
  • Preposition - Shows the relation of words
  • Conjunction - Joins words or sentences
  • Interjection - Shows sudden feelings

Ali is a good student. He works hard and studies every day.

Essay

Time is Money

Time is one of the most valuable things in our life. It is often said that “Time is Money” because once time is lost, it can never be brought back. Money can be earned again, but lost time can never return.

Time teaches us the importance of discipline and responsibility. A successful person always understands the value of time and uses it wisely. Students should make a proper timetable for their studies, rest, games, and other activities.

Wasting time is a bad habit. Spending too much time on unnecessary activities can affect our studies and future. We should avoid laziness and complete our work on time. Every minute of our life is precious.

Time management helps us achieve our goals. If we use our time properly, we can learn new skills, improve our knowledge, and become successful in life.

In conclusion, time is a precious gift from Allah. We should value every moment and use it wisely. We must remember that time once lost never comes back.

“Time is precious, so use it wisely.”

Speech

The Role of Youth in Nation Building

Good morning respected teacher and my dear fellows.

Today, I am going to speak on the topic “The Role of Youth in Nation Building.”

Youth are the future of every nation. They have energy, talent, creativity, and the ability to bring positive changes to society. A strong and successful nation depends greatly on educated, responsible, and hardworking young people.

Education is one of the most important ways through which young people can serve their country. They should gain knowledge, develop useful skills, and use their abilities for the betterment of society.

Young people can play an important role by being honest, responsible, and disciplined. They can help their communities, protect the environment, promote peace, and use modern technology in positive ways.

The youth of Pakistan have great potential. If they work hard with determination and use their talents wisely, they can contribute to the progress of education, science, technology, business, sports, and many other fields.

In conclusion, the youth are not only the future of a nation but also an important part of its present. Every young person should understand their responsibilities and work with honesty, unity, and dedication.

Thank You!
